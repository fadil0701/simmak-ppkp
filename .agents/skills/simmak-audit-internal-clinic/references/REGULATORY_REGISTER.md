# Regulatory Register and Verification Protocol

## Important
This register is a verification workflow, not a statement that any instrument is current or exhaustive. Verify the latest applicable requirements on authoritative Kemenkes/JDIH sources before approving production checklist content.

## Initial leads to verify
- KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/1983/2022 concerning clinic accreditation standards — verify current status, amendments/replacement, official document and applicability.
- Permenkes No. 34 Tahun 2022 — verify title, scope, current validity, amendments/replacement, and applicability to the intended process.

Do not treat these leads as final legal advice or proof of current applicability.

## Register fields
- Regulation/instrument number and official title.
- Issuing authority, publication/establishment date, effective date/status.
- Official source URL/file and retrieval date.
- Version, amendment/replacement relationships, applicable clause/item.
- Reviewer, review date, verification notes, and approval decision.
- Link to instrument version/checklist items that use it.

## Status
- `UNVERIFIED`: source/current status not checked; draft only.
- `UNDER_REVIEW`: source located but applicability/review pending.
- `VERIFIED`: official source, status, and applicability reviewed by designated reviewer.
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced or no longer applicable; preserve historical linkage.

## Rules
- Never invent clauses, element numbers, scoring, thresholds, or legal claims.
- Distinguish official requirement from PPKP operational policy and software design choice.
- Preserve versions used by past audits; do not silently rewrite historical criteria.
- Record who verified the source and when. If authoritative source unavailable, keep in draft and request owner/legal review.
